# Motion recovered from footage

`recovered-dash/` was written by Muse Spark Open from tracker measurements of gameplay footage. It contains motion numbers and generated code. It contains no frames, art, sound or original code from the game.

The footage was of [Dashy Dango](../targets/dashy-dango/README.md) by samX500 and willGuimont, declared ISC. See its [notice](../targets/dashy-dango/NOTICE.txt).
