# RDSTR Stunts recipient

Pinned from [sebayaki/roadster](https://github.com/sebayaki/roadster), revision `8486687c6c885d49231744d5d9434ac4fe21f951`, under its MIT license. Upstream bytes and file hashes are preserved in `upstream/` and `recipient.lock.json`. The game, procedural car, venue, sound, controls and native physics are upstream work, not our hackathon contribution. Its own notice identifies it as an unofficial fan game, unaffiliated with Tesla.

The normal stunt travels in the world X/Y plane: X is the jump path, Y is vertical. Its 240 Hz arcade simulation and scripted driver make before/after checks possible. Our integration adds separately authored host hooks and a Meta-generated adapter around the unchanged recovered dash. It does not recover Tesla firmware or model a physical vehicle modification.
