    How was this dash recovered?SAVED EVIDENCE + SUPPORTED RULES

    From compiled instructions to reusable source

    Meta models received selected WebAssembly instructions and measured donor traces. They reconstructed a standalone controller, which was checked against the actual cartridge. The component runs without the donor's binary, renderer, or assets.

    Loading saved recovery results…

    Generation and repair history · Binary and numeric evidence · Acceptance results

    What is supported?

    • An eight-tick dash covering 60 donor pixels.
    • Direction stays fixed during the dash.
    • Recharge lasts 25 inactive ticks; holding dash can retrigger it.
    • Initial readiness takes 10 ticks. Timing runs at 60 updates per second.

    Verified for the default donor configuration and source body width 8. Combat, scoring, visuals, and full-game equivalence are outside this recovery's claim.

    Human assistance and adapter policies

    A human selected the motion boundary, bytecode functions, memory watches, and development experiments, and authored the checks. Meta Wonder generated the controller; Meta Spark repaired a failed case before fresh acceptance inputs were used. The frozen controller has no manual source repairs. Live investigations choose their own experiments, with world-position and timer readings supplied by a disclosed human-mapped, read-only donor registry.

    PAKU PAKU's and Roadster's adapters were generated by Meta; their host hooks were written by hand. JUJUMP's separate adapter and host hooks were written by hand. All call the exact same frozen component on every logical update. No new recovery runs when you choose a destination. RDSTR Stunts' simulation, models, effects and audio are upstream work by sebayaki; our transfer is an added feature.

    PAKU PAKU preserves its dot-then-ghost collision order, with swept player hitboxes and no collision segment across wraparound. JUJUMP keeps gravity active and sweeps dash movement through the original upward platform-overlap resolution. Roadster bridges 60 Hz component updates to four native 240 Hz substeps and sweeps flight motion through the recipient's collision rules. Contact never resets the recovered timers.
